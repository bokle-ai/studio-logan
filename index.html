The Principal

Yeswanth Loganathan

Founder & Principal Designer

"Design, at its essence, is about how a space makes you feel and the experience it creates."

With years shaping luxury experiences across three continents, Yeswanth brings the strategic rigor of coordinating over $2 billion in global hospitality and commercial projects with the intimate attention required for creating deeply personal homes. Trained at Pratt Institute, New York, and refined through Jeffrey Beers International, BOKA Powell and Studio Fauve — with work for Marriott, Ritz-Carlton, Hard Rock and Disney.

02 — Selected Work

Selected Projects

Five environments across hospitality, residential and commercial — each a study in how heritage and vision hold together.

01 / 05

Canter Hotel

Marriott Signature · Oklahoma · 180 keys

Ranch heritage reimagined for contemporary hospitality — stone, reclaimed timber and handcrafted metalwork, rustic warmth held with sophisticated restraint.

02 / 05

Hard Rock

Athens, Greece · 1,100 keys

On the historic Hellinikon site — the first integrated resort of its kind in continental Europe. A fluid tower where modern luxury is rooted in ancient heritage.

03 / 05

Private Residence

Brooklyn, New York

An 1880s brownstone with contemporary ease and French discipline — spaces designed for how people actually live, not how shelter magazines say they should.

04 / 05

Private Residence

Manhattan · 432 Park Avenue

Private observatories suspended in the sky. Materials respond to daylight — stone warms to gold at sunrise, plaster glows silver at dusk.

05 / 05

Klein Tools

Office & Workplace · Texas

Industrial heritage reframed as a warm, human workplace — exposed brick and steel softened around a double-height atrium.
