Pier Top

Fort Lauderdale · Florida

A rooftop hospitality concept across two levels of a Fort Lauderdale high-rise hotel. PierTop is designed as a place of arrival as much as a bar. Guests step off the elevator into a space that announces itself through craft and light before the Atlantic view registers. That sequencing was deliberate: we wanted the room to matter independently of what it looks out onto.

The main lounge wraps around a central service core, with continuous banquette seating positioned to catch the shifting quality of the coastal light through the afternoon and into dusk. Materials throughout are warm but not heavy: wire-brushed teak, hand-laid zellige tile behind the bar, and a ceiling treatment in rattan that softens the acoustic profile of the room without reading as decorative.

The upper level houses All-Day, a separate dining concept running from breakfast through late service. The interiors there take a different register: brighter, more linear, with whitewashed plaster walls that perform differently at each hour as the light from the east and west windows changes angle. Completed 2024.
