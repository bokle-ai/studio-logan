Pier Top

Fort Lauderdale  ·  Florida

A rooftop bar suspended above Fort Lauderdale's coastline. The design speaks to the drama of the sea and the warmth of the Florida sun — an experience as much as a space.

TypeHospitality
ScaleMixed-Use
ScopeInteriors · Concept
LocationFort Lauderdale, FL
StatusCompleted

Warm leather seating, burnished brass, and panoramic glass walls that dissolve the boundary between interior and horizon. The Pier Top experience begins the moment the elevator doors open.
