02 — Brand Philosophy

Where Heritage
Meets Vision

Studio Logan is rooted in the extraordinary architectural layering of Tamil Nadu — centuries of Dravidian craftsmanship, colonial refinement, and global modernism converging into a singular creative vocabulary.

We do not design for a moment. We design for inheritance. Every material chosen, every spatial proportion considered, every detail resolved speaks to a longer conversation between what was built before us and what endures after us. Our work is a translation — of culture, of memory, of ambition — into the language of built form.

From hospitality environments that redefine arrival to residences that hold a family's story with quiet authority, Studio Logan brings the same philosophical rigour to every scale of project.

The Studio Ethos

Precision without coldness. Grandeur without excess. We believe the most powerful spaces are those that feel inevitable — as if they could not have existed any other way, in any other place.

Our process begins with listening. To the land, to the client, to the culture. Then we bring the full depth of our heritage — Tamil Nadu's ornamental rigour, New York's cosmopolitan clarity — to bear on every decision.

03 — Founder

Loganathan
Yeswanth

Founder & Principal Designer

"Every space carries a memory before the first stone is laid. Our work is to honour that — and to make the new memory worthy of what came before."

Loganathan Yeswanth founded Studio Logan with a singular conviction: that the deepest design intelligence emerges from cultural inheritance, not trend. Raised in Tamil Nadu's temple cities and educated across India and the United States, he brings a dual fluency — the ancient sculptural grammar of Dravidian architecture and the precision of contemporary global practice — to every project.

His work spans Marriott Signature hotels, Disney-affiliated hospitality developments, Ritz-Carlton resort interiors, and a growing body of private residential commissions across New York, Europe, and South Asia. He leads each project personally, believing that design of lasting consequence demands the principal's eye at every stage.
