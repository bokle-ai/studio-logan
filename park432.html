Residential · Penthouse · Completed 2023

Private Residence

432 Park Avenue · Manhattan

A private penthouse occupying a full floor at 432 Park Avenue, one of the tallest residential towers in the western hemisphere. The owners came to us with a specific ask: they wanted a home, not a penthouse. The distinction mattered to them. Spaces that feel curated rather than posed, light that shifts rather than performs, rooms with a grain of real life in them.

The full-floor plan was restructured to break the tyranny of the view. Primary living spaces turn slightly inward, and a gallery corridor running the full depth of the floor creates a procession before the Midtown panorama is revealed at the far end. The reveal is earned. Materials throughout are restrained: honed Ceppo di Gre stone, bleached ceruse oak, and a custom bronze hardware system fabricated in Florence. The kitchen is dressed as a room rather than a utility space, with a larder, a separate scullery, and a long dining table that extends into the south-facing windows.

Completed 2023.
