Marriott Signature · Hospitality · Completed 2023

Canter Hotel

Oklahoma City · United States

A Marriott Signature hotel anchored in the urban core of Oklahoma City. The brief called for a hotel that could feel rooted in the city while reaching a guest who travels with discernment. We built the lobby around the idea of the great hall reimagined through a local material lens: warm walnut, hand-thrown ceramics from Oklahoma artisans, and a lighting installation that reads as a prairie constellation after dark.

The ballroom is the standout, a 6,000 sq ft event space with a coffered ceiling that draws sound into intimacy without sacrificing scale. Guest rooms carry the same language inward: layered textiles, a restrained palette of tobacco and sand, and bespoke joinery that takes the place of the generic hotel case goods that typically flatten a property at this key count.

Completed in 2023 ahead of schedule. The property opened to full occupancy within its first month.
