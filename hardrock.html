Integrated Resort · Opening 2026

Hard Rock Athens

Hellinikon · Athens, Greece

One of Europe's most significant hospitality developments, sited on the Hellinikon waterfront at the edge of Athens. This project sits at a genuine crossroads: a globally recognised brand in one of the world's oldest cities, on land that carries both archaeological and political weight. The design response draws from the geological bedrock of Attica, travertine, raw concrete, textured lime plasters, layered against the Rock aesthetic's confidence and energy.

The 1,100-key property encompasses a full-scale lobby arrival sequence, all-day dining, a fine dining concept with sea views, a beach club integrated into the landscaped coastal edge, and a suite collection positioned at the upper floors to command the Saronic Gulf panorama. The suite interiors take their cue from the classical proportion systems of the Attic plain, reinterpreted through contemporary craft.

Opening in 2026. Studio Logan led interior architecture and FF&E direction across all public areas and the full suite collection.
