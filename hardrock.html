Hard Rock Athens

Athens  ·  Greece

Where music meets Mediterranean luxury. Studio Logan brought rhythm into the architecture — from the lobby's kinetic energy to the quiet opulence of Kuro's private dining rooms.

TypeHospitality
Scale500+ Keys
ScopeInteriors · FF&E
LocationAthens, Greece
StatusCompleted

The Hard Rock brand's energy was translated into a language of scale and warmth — dramatic pendant lights, curated music memorabilia, and a bar that anchors the entire hotel experience. Every surface carries intention, every corner a story worth discovering.

From the kinetic energy of the lobby to the hushed opulence of Kuro's private dining rooms, Studio Logan wove rhythm into every material choice. Leather, brass, and deep lacquer surfaces speak in a register that is both globally relevant and locally rooted.
